Method

What this watches. A user-declared crypto holdings list (BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT — manual mode: quantities are maintained by the owner, not read from an exchange account). Prices refresh hourly from Binance spot USDT klines via Alva data skills; every number on this page is read from the feed at view time.

Alert rules in force (preset: normal) — alerts fire on state changes, never on states, and repeats are suppressed by a novelty gate:

  • 24h move ≥ 3× that asset's own 20-day daily volatility (critical at 6×)
  • 1h fast move ≥ 8%
  • Portfolio drawdown crossing −5/−10/−15/−20/−30% bands from the 30d high
  • Top position crossing 40% of NAV (info)
  • Escalation re-alerts; recovery updates this page silently

Failure honesty. An asset whose price fetch fails is carried at its last known value and flagged (never silently zeroed), its alerts are suppressed, and the Data Status pill above degrades to partial or stale. Assets with no traded pair are excluded from NAV with an explicit "unpriced" status.

Known blind spots. Manual holdings drift until the owner updates the list; news/materiality alerts are disabled in this demo build; USDT is valued at its quote-unit 1.00 (external depeg reference not wired); cost-basis P&L is intentionally absent — changes are period-based, never fabricated from a guessed entry price.
