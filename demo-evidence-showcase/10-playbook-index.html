Method

What this watches. A concentrated, declared book — 800 NVDA, 200 TSM, 100 QQQ, 0.8 BTC, $40k cash, with the owner's target weights (40/15/20/10/15) — a fictional showcase persona; quantities are user-maintained, not read from an account. Hourly refresh, 24/7 (the crypto sleeve sets the clock); equity judgments happen only inside US market sessions. Every number is read from the feed at view time.

Risk lives in exposures, not tickers. Per-asset β and residual are computed against QQQ — the benchmark the owner already holds. When the correlated equity complex moves together, that is one factor event and sends one portfolio alert; a single name only speaks when its residual (its own move, market subtracted) is ≥ 3× its residual σ. Effective bets (1/Σw²) tracks how many independent bets the book really runs — five positions currently behave like ~3.3.

Alert rules in force (preset: normal; every alert names the standing decision it informs, never a trade instruction):

  • Systematic move: ≥2 correlated equities ≥3σ same direction, avg corr ≥0.5 → one portfolio alert
  • Residual move ≥3× residual σ → "re-check this name's thesis"
  • Weight outside its target ± 5pp band → "rebalance decision point" (re-arms 1pp inside the band)
  • Drawdown bands −5/10/15/20/30% in episodes: entry and deepening notify, recovery re-arms silently
  • Effective bets < 2.0 → diversification review; earnings within 3 days; close-run volume ≥3× 20d avg; BTC 24h ≥3σ and 1h fast moves

The replay found the noise first. The 12-month rule replay above initially showed band-edge oscillation (58 alert-days); adding hysteresis re-arm cut it to 31 (~2.6/month) before any user was ever pinged. That is what the replay is for.

Known blind spots. News/materiality alerts disabled in this build; replay covers close-run rules only (no intraday/gap/earnings); holdings are owner-maintained and drift with real trades until updated; single-benchmark residuals (QQQ) — no multi-factor model, by design. Not investment advice.
