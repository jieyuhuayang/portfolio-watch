Portfolio Alerts — Off

Portfolio-level alerts (total value, drawdown, concentration) are off because no position sizes were declared — this watch knows what you hold, not how much. Nothing here is estimated or assumed: an invented portfolio value would be a fabricated number wearing a chart. To unlock the portfolio tier, tell the agent your share counts ("I hold 20 NVDA") or connect an account — the watch upgrades in place, no rebuild.

Method

What this watches. A bare watchlist — NVDA, TSLA, AAPL — built from one sentence, with no brokerage connection and no quantities. US equity prices refresh every 30 minutes across the US trading session (pre-open through the close, weekdays) from Alva's stock kline data; earnings dates come from the earnings calendar. Every number on this page is read from the feed at view time.

Alert rules in force (preset: normal) — alerts fire on state changes, never on states, and repeats are suppressed by a novelty gate:

  • Move vs prior close ≥ 3× that stock's own 20-trading-day volatility (critical at 6×)
  • Opening gap ≥ 2× its 20-day volatility — judged once, at the open
  • Day volume ≥ 3× its 20-day average — judged once, at the close (intraday volume extrapolation is noise)
  • Earnings within 3 days (one heads-up per report date)
  • Escalation re-alerts; recovery updates this page silently

Market-hours honesty. When the US market is closed the badge reads market closed, not stale — a quiet weekend is by design. The cron window is encoded in UTC, so around daylight-saving transitions the first/last run of the day can shift by an hour; the session detector inside the script keeps judgments aligned to actual trading bars.

Known blind spots. News/materiality alerts are disabled in this demo build; after-hours moves are caught at the next session's open-gap check rather than in real time; the watchlist covers exactly the tickers declared — the owner edits the list to change coverage; portfolio-tier analytics stay off until quantities exist (see the panel above — nothing is assumed).
